Iron Man

Anthony Edward Stark. Engineer, arms dealer, Avenger, in that order.

At a glance

Universe
Marvel Cinematic Universe
First appearance
Iron Man (2008)
Species
Human
Role
Founding Avenger
Base
Malibu, then Stark Tower
Power

Stark is the rare hero whose power is entirely removable. Take the suit away and what is left is the part that actually matters: a man who can look at a problem for an afternoon and come back with a working answer.

Backstory

Tony inherits Stark Industries young and runs it the way he runs everything else — brilliantly, and without asking many questions. A weapons demonstration in Afghanistan ends with him wounded, captured, and looking at his own company's ordnance pointed back at him.

He builds the first armour out of scrap in captivity, escapes, and shuts down the weapons division on the same day he gets home. The suits that follow are a long, expensive apology, and he knows it.

Abilities

  • Powered armour. Flight, strength, repulsors and a suit that gets rebuilt after every fight it barely survives.
  • Engineering. The real superpower. He designs faster than most organisations can approve a budget.
  • Arc reactor. A clean power source small enough to sit in his chest, originally built to keep him alive.
  • J.A.R.V.I.S. An AI that runs the suit, the house, and a fair share of the sarcasm.

Personality

Loud, funny and allergic to being handled. He deflects with jokes long past the point where anyone still finds them funny, and the deflection is usually load-bearing.

He is also the one who stays up running the numbers on the thing nobody else wants to think about. Most of his worst decisions come from trying to solve a problem before it arrives.

Why he made the list

A lot of heroes are born into their abilities. Stark builds his, iterates on them, and breaks them constantly — which is closer to how actual work feels than any origin story involving a lucky accident.
